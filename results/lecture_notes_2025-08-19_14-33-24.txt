**Week 1: Understanding LLM-Content Benchmarking Datasets**

**Introduction to GLUE Benchmark**
- The General Language Understanding Evaluation (GLUE) benchmark is a collection of diverse datasets used to evaluate natural language understanding systems, including Large Language Models (LLMs).
- GLUE tasks include text classification, sentiment analysis, and question-answering, providing a comprehensive evaluation platform for LLMs.
- Significance: GLUE benchmark assesses LLM performance in various natural language understanding tasks, aiding in model comparison and improvement.

**GLUE Tasks: Text Classification**
- Text classification task in GLUE involves categorizing text into predefined classes based on content.
- Example: Classifying movie reviews as positive or negative sentiment.
- Learning Objective: Understand how LLMs classify text into distinct categories.

**GLUE Tasks: Sentiment Analysis**
- Sentiment analysis task in GLUE focuses on determining the sentiment (positive, negative, neutral) of text.
- Example: Analyzing product reviews to identify customer sentiments.
- Learning Objective: Recognize the importance of sentiment analysis in natural language processing.

**GLUE Tasks: Question-Answering**
- Question-answering task in GLUE requires LLMs to provide accurate answers to posed questions.
- Example: Answering factual questions based on given text passages.
- Learning Objective: Understand the challenges and capabilities of LLMs in question-answering tasks.
