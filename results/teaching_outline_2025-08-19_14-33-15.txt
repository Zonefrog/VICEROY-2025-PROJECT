     - Learning objectives: Understand the purpose and tasks of the GLUE benchmark in benchmarking natural language understanding systems.
     - Estimated slides: 5
     - Lab activities: Analyze sample tasks from the GLUE benchmark and evaluate LLM performance.
     - Real-world applications: Assessing the performance of LLMs in various natural language understanding tasks.

  2. CommonsenseQA Dataset
     - Explanation: Overview of the CommonsenseQA dataset, focusing on its role in evaluating LLMs in common sense reasoning tasks.
     - Learning objectives: Recognize the importance of evaluating LLMs in understanding and generating common sense reasoning.
     - Estimated slides: 3
     - Lab activities: Solve multiple-choice questions from the CommonsenseQA dataset and analyze LLM performance.
     - Real-world applications: Enhancing LLMs' understanding of everyday situations for better real-world applications.

Week 2: Advancing LLM-Content Detection
Main Topic: LLM-Content Detection
- Sub-topics:
  1. Deep Learning Techniques for LLM-Content Detection
     - Explanation: Introduction to deep learning techniques used for LLM-content detection, focusing on neural networks' role in distinguishing between human-written and machine-generated text.
     - Learning objectives: Understand how deep learning techniques enhance LLM-content detection accuracy.
     - Estimated slides: 6
     - Lab activities: Implement a deep learning model for LLM-content detection using a labeled dataset.
     - Real-world applications: Preventing the spread of misinformation and hate speech through accurate LLM-content detection.

  2. BERT-based Models in LLM-Content Detection
     - Explanation: Overview of BERT-based models' effectiveness in LLM-content detection tasks through fine-tuning on labeled datasets.
     - Learning objectives: Explore the capabilities of BERT-based models in identifying generated or manipulated content.
     - Estimated slides: 4
     - Lab activities: Fine-tune a pre-trained BERT model on a dataset for LLM-content detection and evaluate its performance.
     - Real-world applications: Enhancing the accuracy of LLM-content detection systems using BERT-based models.

Week 3: Strategies for Evading and Detecting LLM Content
Main Topic: Evading LLM Detectors
- Sub-topics:
  1. Adversarial Attacks on LLM Content Detectors
     - Explanation: Understanding how adversarial attacks manipulate input text to evade LLM detectors and the challenges they pose.
     - Learning objectives: Identify strategies used in adversarial attacks to bypass LLM detectors.
     - Estimated slides: 5
     - Lab activities: Create adversarial inputs to test the robustness of LLM detectors.
     - Real-world applications: Understanding the vulnerabilities of LLM detectors and the need for robust detection mechanisms.

  2. Countermeasures Against Evading LLM Detectors
     - Explanation: Overview of techniques such as ensemble learning, feature engineering, and anomaly detection to enhance LLM content detection.
     - Learning objectives: Explore methods to improve the robustness of LLM detectors against adversarial attacks.
     - Estimated slides: 4
     - Lab activities: Implement countermeasures against evading LLM detectors using different techniques.
     - Real-world applications: Strengthening LLM content detection systems to mitigate adversarial attacks effectively.
