Main Topic Title: LLM-Content Detection

Sub-Topics:
2.1 Evading LLM Detectors
2.2 Plagiarism Detection in LLM-Generated Content

Brief Explanation:
- Evading LLM detectors involves techniques such as text manipulation to bypass detection algorithms.
- Plagiarism detection in LLM-generated content utilizes N-gram analysis and machine learning to identify copied text.
- Incorporating domain-specific knowledge enhances the relevance and accuracy of LLM-generated content.

Learning Objectives:
- Explore strategies for evading LLM detectors and understanding their implications.
- Understand the challenges and techniques involved in detecting plagiarism in LLM-generated content.
- Learn how to integrate domain-specific knowledge to improve the quality of LLM-generated text.

Estimated Number of Slides: 15

Lab Activities:
- Students will experiment with different evasion techniques to understand how LLM detectors can be bypassed.
- Students will analyze sample LLM-generated texts to detect instances of plagiarism using N-gram analysis.
- Students will fine-tune a pre-trained LLM model on domain-specific data and compare the quality of generated content.

Real-World Applications and Examples:
- Businesses use LLM detectors to identify and prevent the spread of misinformation and fake news.
- Plagiarism detection in LLM-generated content is essential for academic institutions to maintain academic integrity and originality in research publications.
