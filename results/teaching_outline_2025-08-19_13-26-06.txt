TEACHING OUTLINE:
Teaching Plan for Senior Undergraduate Students:

Week 1: Large Language Models (LLMs) Content Detection

Topic 1: LLM-Content Benchmarking Datasets
- Sub-topics:
  1.1 Webis-20 Benchmark Dataset
  1.2 Toxic Comment Classification Challenge
  1.3 GPT-3 Benchmark Dataset
- Explanation: This topic covers benchmark datasets used for evaluating LLMs in content detection tasks, including a diverse range of text samples labeled for toxicity, threats, insults, and hate speech.
- Learning Objectives: Understand the importance of benchmark datasets in evaluating LLM performance, identify key datasets used in content detection tasks, and analyze the characteristics of different benchmark datasets.
- Estimated Slides: 10
- Lab Activities: Analyze sample text data from benchmark datasets, train LLMs on benchmark datasets, and evaluate model performance.
- Real-world Applications: Improving content moderation algorithms, enhancing online safety measures, and developing more accurate toxicity detection systems.

Topic 2: LLM-Content Detection
- Sub-topics:
  2.1 Challenges in Detecting LLM-Generated Content
  2.2 BERT-Based Model for Content Detection
  2.3 Transformer Models for LLM-Content Detection
- Explanation: This topic delves into the challenges of detecting LLM-generated content, the use of BERT-based models for high accuracy detection, and the effectiveness of transformer models in identifying LLM-generated text.
- Learning Objectives: Identify the unique challenges in detecting LLM-generated content, understand the role of BERT-based models in content detection, and analyze the benefits of transformer models in LLM-content detection tasks.
- Estimated Slides: 15
- Lab Activities: Implement a BERT-based content detection model, fine-tune transformer models for content detection, and compare the performance of different detection algorithms.
- Real-world Applications: Enhancing fake news detection systems, improving content filtering mechanisms, and developing robust content moderation tools.

Week 2: Evading LLM Detectors and Watermarking LLM Content

Topic 3: Evading LLM Detectors
- Sub-topics:
  3.1 Evasion Attacks on Language Models
  3.2 Adversarial Attacks on LLMs
  3.3 Evasion Techniques for LLM Detectors
- Explanation: This topic explores evasion attacks on LLMs, adversarial attacks to deceive models, and evasion techniques to bypass detection mechanisms.
- Learning Objectives: Understand the concept of evasion attacks on LLMs, identify different adversarial attack strategies, and analyze evasion techniques used to deceive LLM detectors.
- Estimated Slides: 12
- Lab Activities: Create adversarial examples to evade LLM detectors, analyze the impact of evasion attacks on model performance, and develop countermeasures against adversarial attacks.
- Real-world Applications: Strengthening cybersecurity measures, enhancing model robustness against attacks, and improving AI system security.

Topic 4: Watermarking LLM Content
- Sub-topics:
  4.1 Techniques for Watermarking LLM Content
  4.2 Applications of Watermarking LLM Content
- Explanation: This topic covers techniques for embedding unique identifiers into LLM-generated text, such as invisible watermarking, visible watermarking, and robust watermarking, along with real-world applications.
- Learning Objectives: Explore different watermarking techniques for LLM content, understand the significance of watermarking in content protection, and analyze the applications of watermarking in LLM-generated text.
- Estimated Slides: 8
- Lab Activities: Implement watermarking techniques on LLM-generated text, assess the effectiveness of different watermarking methods, and discuss the importance of content protection in various industries.
- Real-world Applications: Protecting intellectual property rights, preventing content plagiarism, and verifying the authenticity of generated text.

By following this teaching plan, students will gain a comprehensive understanding of LLM-content detection, evasion techniques, and watermarking strategies, preparing them for real-world applications in content moderation, cybersecurity, and intellectual property protection.
