Explanation: Compliance involves agreeing to a request or suggestion from another person. This can be influenced by techniques such as reciprocity, scarcity, and authority. Compliance is commonly used in marketing, sales, and social interactions to persuade individuals to take specific actions.

- Recognize the ethical implications of using compliance techniques in various contexts.

- Compliance techniques can be powerful tools for persuading individuals to take specific actions.
