TEACHING OUTLINE:
Week 1: Introduction to Corporate Social Responsibility

Main Topic Title: Understanding Corporate Social Responsibility (CSR)

- Definition of CSR: Introducing the concept of CSR as a business model that helps companies be socially accountable to themselves, stakeholders, and the public.
- Importance of CSR: Exploring the benefits of CSR for businesses, including building a positive reputation, enhancing customer loyalty, attracting talent, and contributing to sustainable development.
- Strategies for Implementing CSR: Discussing common strategies for implementing CSR, such as integrating CSR into the core business strategy and engaging with stakeholders.

Learning Objectives:
- Define corporate social responsibility and its significance for businesses.
- Identify key strategies for implementing CSR within organizations.
- Understand the importance of engaging with stakeholders in CSR initiatives.

Lab Activities/Practical Exercises:
- Conduct a case study analysis of a company known for its successful CSR initiatives.
- Role-play stakeholder engagement scenarios to practice effective communication strategies.
Real-World Applications and Examples:
- Examining real-world examples of companies that have successfully integrated CSR into their business strategies.
- Discussing current CSR trends in various industries and their impact on business operations.
