- Sub-topics:
  2.1 Coherence and consistency analysis for content detection
  2.2 Language patterns and vocabulary examination
  2.3 Comparison of genuine vs. generated content

Explanation: This topic will cover techniques used for detecting LLM-generated content, focusing on analyzing coherence, language patterns, and vocabulary to differentiate between genuine and generated text.

Learning Objectives:
- Understand how coherence and consistency analysis can help in detecting LLM-generated content
- Identify key language patterns and vocabulary used in LLM-generated text
- Differentiate between genuine and generated content using detection techniques

Estimated Number of Slides: 12
Lab Activities:
- Analyze sample texts to identify inconsistencies and incoherencies that may indicate generated content
- Compare language patterns and vocabulary usage in genuine and generated text

Real-World Applications:
- Applying content detection techniques to identify and prevent the spread of misinformation and fake news generated by LLMs

Week 2: Evading LLM Detectors and Watermarking LLM Content

  3.3 Adversarial attacks on LLM detectors

Topic 4: Watermarking LLM Content

Explanation: This topic will cover digital watermarking techniques for protecting LLM-generated content, emphasizing the importance of safeguarding intellectual property through watermarking.

Learning Objectives:
- Understand how digital watermarking can protect LLM content
- Identify the significance of watermarking in preserving intellectual property rights
- Explore practical applications of watermarking in LLM-generated content
