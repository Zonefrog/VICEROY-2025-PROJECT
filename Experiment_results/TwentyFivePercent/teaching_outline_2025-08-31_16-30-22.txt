TEACHING OUTLINE:
Week 1: Ancient Greek Theater

Main Topic: Origins and Development of Ancient Greek Theater
- Sub-topics:
  1. Historical background of Ancient Greek theater
     - Explanation of how theater originated in Athens around the 6th century BC and its significance in ancient Greek culture.
     - Reference to the religious festivals where plays were performed to honor the gods.
  
  2. Genres of Greek Drama: Tragedy and Comedy
     - Overview of the two main genres of Greek drama, tragedy, and comedy.
     - Explanation of how tragedy plays focused on serious themes and emotions, while comedy plays were more lighthearted.
  
  3. Role of Masks in Ancient Greek Theater
     - Discussion on the importance of masks in Greek theater performances.
     - Reference to how masks were used by actors to portray different characters and emotions, as well as amplify their voices in outdoor theaters.

- Learning Objectives:
  - Understand the historical origins and significance of Ancient Greek theater.
  - Differentiate between the genres of tragedy and comedy in Greek drama.
  - Recognize the role of masks in Ancient Greek theater performances.

- Estimated Number of Slides: 15
- Lab Activities:
  - Students can create their own Greek theater masks using basic materials.
- Real-world Applications:
  - Understanding the influence of Ancient Greek theater on modern theatrical practices.

Main Topic: Influence of Roman Theater on Modern Performances
- Sub-topics:
  1. Elaborate Productions in Roman Theater
     - Overview of the grandiose theatrical productions in the Roman Empire.
     - Discussion on the use of large-scale sets, intricate costumes, and special effects in Roman theater.

  2. Legacy of Roman Theater in Modern Performances
     - Analysis of how Roman theater elements have influenced modern theater practices.
     - Reference to the continued use of masks in modern theater to portray different characters.

  3. Evolution of Theater Spaces
     - Comparison of ancient open-air theaters to modern indoor theaters.
     - Reference to the development of amphitheaters and other theater spaces over time.

- Learning Objectives:
  - Recognize the influence of Roman theater on modern theatrical productions.
  - Understand the evolution of theater spaces from ancient times to the present day.
  - Analyze the legacy of Roman theater in contemporary performances.

- Estimated Number of Slides: 20
- Lab Activities:
  - Students can design a modern theatrical production incorporating elements inspired by Roman theater.
- Real-world Applications:
  - Applying Roman theater techniques to enhance modern theatrical performances.
