COMPREHENSIVE LECTURE NOTES:
Lecture Notes: Week 1 - Understanding the Gender Pay Gap

Main Topic Title: Factors Contributing to the Gender Pay Gap

Learning Objectives:
- Understand the various factors that contribute to the gender pay gap
- Recognize the impact of occupational segregation and the motherhood penalty on women's earnings
- Analyze the role of negotiation skills in perpetuating the gender pay gap

I. Occupational Segregation
- Occupational segregation refers to the phenomenon where women are concentrated in lower-paying jobs compared to men due to societal norms and expectations.
- Example: Women are often overrepresented in caregiving roles such as nursing or teaching, which are traditionally lower-paying professions.
- Impact: This disparity in job distribution contributes to the overall gender pay gap as women are more likely to earn less than men in these roles.

II. Motherhood Penalty
- The motherhood penalty describes the negative impact on women's earnings when they take time off for caregiving responsibilities, particularly after becoming mothers.
- Example: Studies have shown that mothers are often perceived as less committed to their jobs and are passed over for promotions or pay raises.
- Impact: This penalty can result in long-term financial repercussions for women, affecting their earning potential and career advancement opportunities.

III. Negotiation Skills
- Differences in negotiation skills between men and women can also contribute to the gender pay gap, with women often being less assertive in negotiating for higher salaries or promotions.
- Example: Research has shown that women are less likely to negotiate for higher starting salaries when offered a job compared to men.
- Impact: This lack of negotiation skills can lead to women earning less over their careers compared to their male counterparts.

Real-World Examples:
- Case Study: Company X implemented salary transparency policies to address the gender pay gap, leading to increased awareness and accountability for equal pay.
- News Article: Recent initiative by Organization Y to provide bias training for employees to combat gender discrimination in the workplace.

Lab Activities/Practical Exercises:
1. Role-playing exercise: Students will participate in a negotiation simulation to experience the challenges women may face in advocating for equal pay.
2. Data analysis: Students will analyze industry data on occupational segregation to identify trends and propose solutions for promoting gender equality in different sectors.

Key Takeaways:
- Occupational segregation, the motherhood penalty, and negotiation skills all contribute to the gender pay gap.
- Addressing these factors is crucial for achieving gender equality in the workplace.
- Companies can implement policies such as salary transparency and bias training to promote fair pay practices and combat gender discrimination.

Assessment Questions:

3. Why are negotiation skills important in addressing the gender pay gap, and how can women improve their negotiation strategies?

Discussion Points:
1. How can organizations promote equal opportunities for women in traditionally male-dominated industries?
2. What role do government policies play in addressing the gender pay gap and promoting gender equality in the workplace?
3. How can individuals advocate for fair pay practices and combat gender discrimination in their own careers?
