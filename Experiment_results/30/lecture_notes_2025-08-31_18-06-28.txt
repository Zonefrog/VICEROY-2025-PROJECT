- Sub-topics:
  1. Exploratory Research and Preclinical Testing
  2. Clinical Development Phases 1, 2, and 3
  3. Regulatory Review and Approval

Explanation:
- Vaccine development is a complex process that involves several stages from initial research to regulatory approval. 
- Exploratory research and preclinical testing are the first steps in developing a new vaccine. This stage involves laboratory research and testing on cells and animals to assess safety and efficacy.
- Clinical development consists of three phases: Phase 1 focuses on safety and dosage, Phase 2 evaluates efficacy and side effects, and Phase 3 involves large-scale testing in diverse populations.
- Regulatory review and approval are necessary steps to ensure the vaccine meets safety and efficacy standards set by regulatory agencies.

Learning Objectives:
- Understand the sequential stages of vaccine development.
- Recognize the significance of each stage in bringing a vaccine to market.
