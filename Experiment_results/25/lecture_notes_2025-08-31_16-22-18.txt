  - E-books are electronic versions of books that can be read on devices like e-readers, tablets, and smartphones.
  - Blogs are online platforms where writers share their thoughts, stories, and opinions with readers.
  - Social media posts are short pieces of writing shared on social networking sites like Twitter, Instagram, and Facebook.

- Interactive Storytelling and Multimedia Elements in Literature:
  - Interactive storytelling involves engaging readers in the narrative through choices, branching storylines, and multimedia elements.
  - Multimedia elements include images, videos, audio clips, and animations incorporated into the storytelling experience.
  - Examples of interactive storytelling include choose-your-own-adventure books and online interactive fiction.

- Democratization of Online Publishing Platforms:
  - Online publishing platforms like Amazon Kindle Direct Publishing and Wattpad have made it easier for authors to self-publish their work.
  - Self-publishing allows authors to bypass traditional publishing houses and reach a global audience directly.
  - The democratization of online publishing has opened up opportunities for diverse voices and genres to thrive in the literary world.
