  - Prominent artists, writers, architects, and philosophers of the Renaissance include Leonardo da Vinci, known for his iconic paintings like the Mona Lisa and The Last Supper; Michelangelo, renowned for his sculptures such as David and the Sistine Chapel ceiling; William Shakespeare, the playwright behind timeless works like Romeo and Juliet and Hamlet; and Nicolaus Copernicus, the astronomer who proposed a heliocentric model of the universe.
- Sub-Topic 3: Impact of the Renaissance
  - The Renaissance had a lasting influence on art, literature, architecture, philosophy, science, and technology. It marked a shift towards humanism, individualism, and the exploration of the natural world. The period laid the foundation for modern society's values and advancements.
