Sub-Topics:

- Innovations in Techniques and Styles: Students will learn about the advancements in techniques like linear perspective and the use of oil paint by artists during the Renaissance period.
- Influence of Classical Antiquity on Renaissance Art: This sub-topic will explore how artists drew inspiration from ancient Greek and Roman art, incorporating classical themes and styles into their work.

- Appreciating the influence of classical art can deepen one's understanding of art history and cultural heritage.
