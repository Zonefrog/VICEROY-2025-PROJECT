Lecture Notes:

Week 1: Understanding the Gender Pay Gap

Main Topic: Gender Pay Gap

Sub-Topics:
1. Definition and Causes of the Gender Pay Gap
   - The gender pay gap refers to the difference in earnings between men and women in the workplace. It is a persistent issue that affects women across various industries and job levels.
   - Factors contributing to the gender pay gap include discrimination, occupational segregation, and unconscious bias.
   - For example, women often face unequal pay for performing the same work as men, limiting their financial well-being and career advancement opportunities.

2. Global Disparities in Gender Pay Gap
   - The gender pay gap varies by country, with some countries having a larger gap than others.
   - For instance, Iceland has one of the smallest gender pay gaps globally, while countries like South Korea and Japan have larger disparities.
   - Cultural norms, government policies, and workplace practices all contribute to the variations in pay between men and women.

3. Impact of the Gender Pay Gap on Women
   - The gender pay gap negatively affects women's financial well-being and career advancement.
   - Studies have shown that, on average, women earn less than men for the same work, leading to economic disparities.
   - Addressing the gender pay gap is crucial for promoting gender equality in the workplace and empowering women to achieve their full potential.

Real-World Applications and Examples:
- Current initiatives and policies aim to reduce the gender pay gap in various countries, such as equal pay legislation and diversity programs.
- Real-life stories of women who have advocated for equal pay highlight the importance of gender equality in the workplace and the impact of the gender pay gap on individuals.
