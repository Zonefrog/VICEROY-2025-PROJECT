Main Topic Title: Decision Trees and Ensemble Learning
- Sub-Topics:
  3.1 Decision tree structure and working mechanism
  3.2 Random Forest algorithm
  3.3 Benefits of ensemble learning
- Explanation: This section will cover decision trees, their structure, and the concept of ensemble learning using the Random Forest algorithm. Students will understand how combining multiple decision trees can enhance predictive accuracy.
- Learning Objectives: Understand the concept of decision trees and how Random Forest improves on single decision tree models. Recognize the advantages of ensemble learning.
- Estimated Number of Slides: 15

Main Topic Title: Support Vector Machines in Machine Learning
- Sub-Topics:
  4.1 Working principle of Support Vector Machines
  4.2 Hyperplane and margin optimization
  4.3 Applications of SVM in classification and regression
- Explanation: This section will focus on SVM as a powerful supervised learning model for classification and regression tasks. Students will learn how SVM finds the optimal hyperplane to separate data points.

- Estimated Number of Slides: 15
- Lab Activities: Use SVM to classify email messages as spam or non-spam based on text features.
- Real-World Applications: Handwriting recognition in postal services, sentiment analysis in social media, and medical diagnosis.

- Estimated Number of Slides: 20
- Lab Activities: Apply K-means clustering on a dataset to group similar data points together.
- Real-World Applications: Customer segmentation in retail, anomaly detection in cybersecurity, and image compression in multimedia.
