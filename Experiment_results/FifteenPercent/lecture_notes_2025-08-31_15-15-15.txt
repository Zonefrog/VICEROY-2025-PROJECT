I. Overview of Artificial Intelligence in Public Health Crisis Management

II. Applications of AI in Public Health Crisis Management
A. Data Analysis
- AI can analyze large datasets to identify patterns and trends relevant to public health crises.
- Example: AI used to analyze COVID-19 data to predict hotspots and allocate resources efficiently.

B. Predictive Modeling
- AI can develop models to predict disease outbreaks and trends in public health emergencies.
- Example: AI models predicting the spread of infectious diseases like Ebola.

C. Early Detection
- AI tools can assist in early detection of potential health crises by analyzing data in real-time.
- Example: AI used for early detection of outbreaks like Zika virus.

D. Resource Allocation
- AI can optimize resource allocation during public health crises based on data analysis and predictive modeling.
- Example: AI used to allocate medical supplies during natural disasters.
