- Definition of the gender pay gap: The gender pay gap refers to the difference in earnings between men and women in the workplace. Women typically earn around 82 cents for every dollar earned by men, with women of color facing even wider gaps.
- Implications in the workplace: The gender pay gap has significant implications for women, as it can lead to lower lifetime earnings, limited career advancement opportunities, and financial insecurity.
- Factors contributing to the gender pay gap: Occupational segregation, discrimination, lack of pay transparency, and negotiation biases are key factors contributing to the gender pay gap.
- Gender pay gap impact on women of color: Women of color face even greater disparities in earnings, with Black women earning around 63 cents and Latina women earning around 55 cents for every dollar earned by white, non-Hispanic men.
