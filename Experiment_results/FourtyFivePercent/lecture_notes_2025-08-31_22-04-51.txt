   - Explanation: The gender pay gap refers to the average difference in earnings between men and women in the workplace. Equal pay for equal work ensures that individuals are compensated fairly regardless of gender. Policy changes, such as enforcing anti-discrimination laws and implementing affirmative action programs, can also contribute to reducing the gender pay gap.
