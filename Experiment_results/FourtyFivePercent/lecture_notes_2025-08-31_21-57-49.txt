Explanation of AI technology in healthcare:
- Artificial Intelligence (AI) refers to the simulation of human intelligence processes by machines, including learning, reasoning, and self-correction.
- In healthcare, AI technology involves the use of algorithms and machine learning to analyze complex medical data and assist healthcare professionals in making clinical decisions.

Applications of AI in medical diagnostics:
- AI is used in medical diagnostics to interpret medical images, analyze patient data, and predict disease outcomes.
- Machine learning algorithms can identify patterns in medical data that may not be easily recognizable by human clinicians, leading to more accurate diagnoses.

Benefits of AI in improving healthcare outcomes:
- AI technology can help in early disease detection, personalized treatment planning, and improving patient outcomes.
- By streamlining processes and automating tasks, AI can enhance efficiency in healthcare delivery and reduce costs.
