Week 1: Introduction to Gender Pay Gap and Workplace Discrimination

1. Main Topic Title: Understanding the Gender Pay Gap
   - Sub-Topics:
     1.1 Definition and Calculation of Gender Pay Gap
         - This sub-topic will cover the definition and calculation methods of the gender pay gap, focusing on how it is typically measured as the difference in earnings between men and women.
     1.2 Factors Contributing to the Gender Pay Gap
         - This sub-topic will explore the various factors that contribute to the gender pay gap, such as occupational segregation, discrimination, and caregiving responsibilities.
     1.3 Global Variances in Gender Pay Gap
         - This sub-topic will discuss the significant differences in gender pay gap statistics across different countries, using examples from the United States and the United Kingdom.

   - Learning Objectives:
     - Understand the concept and calculation methods of the gender pay gap.
     - Identify key factors contributing to the gender pay gap.
     - Compare and contrast global variances in gender pay gap statistics.

   - Estimated Number of Slides: 15
   - Lab Activities/Practical Exercises: Analyze real-world data on gender pay gap statistics in different countries and calculate the pay gap ratio.
   - Real-World Applications and Examples: Discuss how gender pay gap data can inform policy decisions and organizational practices to promote gender equality in the workplace.

2. Main Topic Title: Workplace Discrimination Against Women
   - Sub-Topics:
     2.1 Forms of Workplace Discrimination
         - This sub-topic will cover various forms of workplace discrimination against women, including unequal pay, lack of promotions, harassment, and exclusion from leadership roles.
     2.2 Gender Bias in the Workplace
         - This sub-topic will explore how gender bias in the workplace can create a hostile environment for women employees, affecting their career advancement and overall well-being.
     2.3 Types of Workplace Discrimination
         - This sub-topic will delve into specific types of discrimination women may face, such as maternal discrimination, sexual harassment, and stereotyping.

   - Learning Objectives:
     - Identify different forms of workplace discrimination against women.
     - Understand the impact of gender bias on women's career progression.
     - Recognize the types of discrimination women may experience in the workplace.

   - Estimated Number of Slides: 20
   - Lab Activities/Practical Exercises: Role-playing scenarios to simulate workplace discrimination situations and discuss strategies for addressing them.
   - Real-World Applications and Examples: Examine case studies of companies that have successfully addressed workplace discrimination issues and improved gender equality outcomes.

Week 2: Gender Stereotypes and the Glass Ceiling

3. Main Topic Title: Gender Stereotypes in the Workplace
   - Sub-Topics:
     3.1 Definition of Gender Stereotypes
         - This sub-topic will define gender stereotypes in the workplace and how they influence hiring, promotion, and treatment of employees based on gender.
     3.2 Impact of Gender Stereotypes
         - This sub-topic will explore the impact of gender stereotypes on women in the workplace, highlighting biases in decision-making processes and opportunities.
     3.3 Common Stereotypes in the Workplace
         - This sub-topic will discuss common stereotypes about women's roles, behaviors, and capabilities that can hinder their professional advancement.

   - Real-World Applications and Examples: Explore how companies have implemented diversity and inclusion programs to combat gender stereotypes and promote a more inclusive work environment.

4. Main Topic Title: The Glass Ceiling Phenomenon
   - Sub-Topics:
     4.1 Definition and Causes of the Glass Ceiling
         - This sub-topic will define the concept of the glass ceiling and examine the factors contributing to barriers for women's advancement in the workplace.
     4.2 Impacts of the Glass Ceiling
         - This sub-topic will explore the impacts of the glass ceiling on women's career trajectories, organizational diversity, and overall business performance.
     4.3 Strategies to Break Through the Glass Ceiling
         - This sub-topic will discuss strategies and best practices for women to overcome the glass ceiling, including mentorship, networking, and skill development.

   - Learning Objectives:
     - Understand the definition and causes of the glass ceiling phenomenon.
     - Evaluate the effects of the glass ceiling on women's career progression and organizational dynamics.
     - Identify effective strategies for breaking through the glass ceiling in the workplace.

   - Estimated Number of Slides: 22
   - Lab Activities/Practical Exercises: Role-playing exercises to simulate scenarios where women encounter barriers to advancement and develop action plans to overcome them.
   - Real-World Applications and Examples: Showcase success stories of women who have shattered the glass ceiling in their respective industries and share lessons learned from their experiences.
