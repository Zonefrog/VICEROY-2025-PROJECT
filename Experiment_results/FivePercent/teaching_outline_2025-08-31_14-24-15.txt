Week 3: Clinical Development of Vaccines

Main Topic: Phases of Clinical Trials
- Sub-Topic 1: Phase I Clinical Trials
  - Explanation: Phase I trials assess the safety and dosage of the vaccine candidate in a small group of healthy volunteers to determine the initial safety profile and potential side effects. (Source: Database - Clinical Development)
- Sub-Topic 2: Phase II Clinical Trials
  - Explanation: Phase II trials expand the study to a larger group of volunteers to evaluate the immunogenicity, optimal dosage, and preliminary efficacy of the vaccine candidate. (Source: Database - Clinical Development)
- Sub-Topic 3: Phase III Clinical Trials
  - Explanation: Phase III trials involve a larger population to confirm the safety, efficacy, and effectiveness of the vaccine candidate compared to a control group, providing robust data for regulatory approval. (Source: Database - Clinical Development)
- Learning Objectives: By the end of this topic, students will be able to differentiate between the phases of clinical trials, understand the objectives of each phase, and recognize the significance of clinical data in vaccine development.
- Estimated Number of Slides: 15
- Lab Activities: Students can analyze clinical trial data from a hypothetical vaccine candidate and discuss the key findings from each phase to evaluate safety, efficacy, and immunogenicity.
- Real-World Applications: Students can explore how clinical trials have led to the approval of vaccines, such as the COVID-19 vaccines, through rigorous testing in different phases to ensure safety and efficacy.
