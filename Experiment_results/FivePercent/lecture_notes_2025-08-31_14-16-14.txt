Evolution of Digital Literature Formats:
- Digital literature encompasses various formats, including e-books, audiobooks, and online articles.
- E-books provide portability and accessibility, changing how readers engage with content.
- Audiobooks offer a hands-free reading experience, catering to auditory learners.

Impact of E-books and Audiobooks on Reading Preferences:
- E-books have made reading more accessible, especially for individuals with visual impairments.
- Audiobooks cater to multitaskers who can listen while performing other activities.
- Research shows that e-books and audiobooks have influenced the way people choose to read.

Accessibility and Convenience of Online Platforms:
- Online platforms like Kindle, Audible, and Scribd offer a wide range of reading materials.
- Readers can access digital literature anytime, anywhere, enhancing convenience.
- Online platforms provide features like annotations and bookmarks for a personalized reading experience.

Changes in Traditional Reading Habits:
- Digital literature has led to a shift from physical books to electronic devices.
- Traditional reading practices, such as visiting libraries, have been replaced by online browsing.
- The rise of digital literature has prompted discussions on the impact of screen time on reading habits.
